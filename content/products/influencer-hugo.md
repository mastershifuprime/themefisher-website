Influencer is a modern, responsive Hugo theme designed for book authors and writers, featuring built-in Snipcart support for payments and optimized performance.

### Key Features

- Google PageSpeed Score 95/100 (Desktop)
- Highly customizable design
- Built-in Snipcart payment support
- Chapter section for book content
- Customer review section
- Structured data with Open Graph and Twitter Cards
- Google Analytics integration
- Contact form support
- GDPR consent enabled
- Optimized images, CSS, and JS via Hugo Pipe
- Netlify settings pre-configured
- Free updates and Lifetime Premium Support
- Detail Documentation.
